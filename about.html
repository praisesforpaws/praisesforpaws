About Praises for Paws 🐾

A student-led initiative bringing people together to make a difference in the lives of animals.

Who We Are

A Community Built on Compassion

Praises for Paws is a student-led nonprofit initiative dedicated to promoting various aspects of pet welfare through advocacy, encouragement of adoption, and fundraising.

We believe every animal deserves to feel loved, safe, and cared for. Through our community, we hope to inspire people to take action and make a positive difference in the lives of pets.

By encouraging adoption and fundraising through social media, public events, and campaigns, we aspire to expand our pet-loving community and create a long-lasting impact on general pet welfare.

What Drives Us

Our Mission

💗
“Our mission is to make every set of paws feel loved and get the basic facilities they need.”

We strive to make this mission a reality by supporting animals, spreading awareness, and bringing together people who share our passion for animal welfare.
