Our story

Meet Praises for Paws ❤️

A student-led initiative built on compassion, community, and a love for animals.

Who We Are

Praises for Paws is a student-led organization dedicated to promoting pet welfare through fundraising, adoption awareness, and advocacy.

🐾

Our Purpose

We want to help animals receive the care, supplies, and support they need while inspiring more people to get involved.

💗

Our Community

We bring together students, families, volunteers, and pet lovers who want to make a positive difference.

🌷

Our Approach

We use public events, social media, fundraising initiatives, and educational campaigns to spread awareness and encourage action.

Why we do it

Our Mission Statement

“To make every set of paws feel loved and get the basic facilities they need.”

By encouraging adoption and fundraising through social media, public events, and campaigns, we aspire to expand our pet-loving community and create a long-lasting impact on general pet welfare.
